That experience helped motivate me to build Ally afterward.”

What’s inside

01 / JOURNEY

A 90-day cannabis-free Journey

Daily education, reflection and self-reported check-ins to give your days some structure. Ninety days is the program’s format, not a promise of a biological reset.

02 / TALK

Private Talk with Ally

An AI companion for lifestyle reflection when you want a place to put your thoughts. We want to learn when it feels useful—and when it doesn’t.

03 / ON YOUR PHONE

On-device AI

After a one-time model download of about 2.6GB and setup, the AI works offline on your Android phone.

A few practical questions

Why do you need my Google Play email?

Google Play Internal Testing requires us to add your exact Google account before you can install Ally. The form asks for your consent first. After we review eligibility and add your account, we email the opt-in instructions.

Do I need an Ally account?

No. Installation uses your Google Play account, but Ally itself does not require an Ally account.

Is Ally treatment or a promise that I’ll quit?

No. Ally is nonmedical lifestyle and self-reflection support. It is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, a medical device or an emergency service. We make no quit-success or clinical efficacy claims.

What happens to my signup information?

Ally uses your confirmations, Play email and optional phone model for beta eligibility, access and follow-up. Responses are not published. Google processes the form under its privacy practices. You may withdraw or request deletion by emailing allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com.

Can I ask a question before joining?

Yes. Email allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com. Please keep sensitive personal history out of signup and support emails.
