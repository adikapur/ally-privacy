Ally Privacy Policy

Updated October 4, 2026

Ally is the developer and operator of this app. Contact allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com for privacy questions, deletion requests, feedback or incidents.

Ally provides lifestyle and recovery education, reflection and a self-reported Journey. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Ally is not an emergency service; contact human emergency or crisis services if you are in immediate danger.

Information on your phone

Ally stores your preferred name, app preferences, Journey progress, check-ins/reflections and Talk conversations in its private app storage to provide these features. No Ally account is required.

Talk message text is encrypted using AES-GCM with a key held by Android Keystore. Journey records, profile information, conversation metadata and app preferences are app-private records; Ally does not encrypt its entire database. Android/device protections also apply. Ally has no separate biometric lock.

After the model is installed, Gemma generates Talk responses on your phone. Conversation and Journey content are not sent to a cloud AI service. Local diagnostics can include response source, model/runtime version, timing, character counts and error types. Ally has no configured remote usage analytics or crash-report upload service. Android and device vendors may separately process operating-system diagnostics under their own settings and policies.

Model downloads and external services

When you request setup, Ally downloads approximately 2.59 GB over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its delivery providers. They receive connection information, including your IP address and the requested model file. Ally does not include your Talk or Journey content in these requests. Their information handling is governed by their own policies, including Hugging Face's privacy policy. Download work can continue or resume in the background after you request it, subject to the connection setting you choose.

Human-support links open external services only when you choose them. A support share action opens another app with a generic support request. You decide whether to send it. External services have their own privacy practices.

Feedback and response reports

Help → Send beta feedback and Report response by email show a draft addressed to allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com before opening your email app. The draft includes Ally's version/build and the comment you enter. Phone model/Android version and one selected Ally response are optional. No entire conversation or Journey record is attached automatically. You choose whether to send the email in your email app.

Opening a draft shares its contents with your chosen email app. That app's storage, draft synchronization and other handling follow its own privacy practices. If you send email, Ally and the email providers receive what you send, including the email address and message information supplied by the email service. Ally uses support information to respond to questions and investigate app problems or unsafe responses. Avoid sending sensitive recovery information unnecessarily. Support messages are stored separately in the support email system; clearing Ally's storage does not delete them. Contact the support address to ask about retention of a specific message or request its deletion. Email-provider handling and copies outside Ally's control remain subject to those providers' policies.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your phone until you delete them. Talk → Chats provides a confirmed Delete all Talk history action that removes saved Talk messages, Talk memory records, saved generation records and older imported Talk chats. Journey and bounded runtime failure counters are retained. Remove downloaded model removes the model, its partial download and its compiled cache; history and Journey remain. Cancel a running download and wait for it to stop before removing the files. Talk requires another model download afterward.

Android Settings → Apps → Ally → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling Ally, removes Ally's private local data, preferences and model files. These actions cannot be undone through Ally. App backup and app-data cloud/device-transfer backup are disabled in this build; Ally offers no cloud restore. A cancelled download may retain a partial file until you resume it, remove the model files, clear storage or uninstall.

Deletion is app-level removal, not a guarantee of forensic erasure of flash storage. It does not erase screenshots, clipboard copies, email or other copies you deliberately shared outside Ally.

Screen, clipboard and notifications

Ally allows screenshots and screen recordings. Your keyboard, screen-access features or someone with device access can expose visible or typed content. Long-pressing a message copies it to the system clipboard. Clipboard protections and copies outside Ally follow Android/other-app behavior.

Optional reminders display generic Journey information according to your notification and lock-screen settings. Ally does not put Talk message text in its reminder notifications.

Changes and contact

Material changes to app data practices require an updated notice and, where appropriate, updated disclosures. Send privacy questions and requests to allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com. Do not use this address for emergency assistance.
