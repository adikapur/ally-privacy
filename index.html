Ally Privacy Policy

Updated October 4, 2026

Ally is the developer and operator of this app. Contact allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com for privacy questions, deletion requests, feedback or incidents.

Ally provides lifestyle and recovery education, reflection and a self-reported Journey. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Ally is not an emergency service; contact human emergency or crisis services if you are in immediate danger.

Information on your phone

Ally stores your preferred name, app preferences, Journey progress, check-ins/reflections and Talk conversations in its private app storage to provide these features. No Ally account is required.

Talk message text is encrypted using AES-GCM with a key held by Android Keystore. Journey records, profile information, conversation metadata and app preferences are app-private records; Ally does not encrypt its entire database. Android/device protections also apply. Ally has no separate biometric lock.

After the model is installed, Gemma generates Talk responses on your phone. Conversation and Journey content are not sent to a cloud AI service. Only an optional, explicitly reviewed response report is sent to the support backend as described below. Local diagnostics can include response source, model/runtime version, timing, character counts and error types. Ally has no configured remote usage analytics or crash-report upload service. Android and device vendors may separately process operating-system diagnostics under their own settings and policies.

Model downloads and external services

When you request setup, Ally downloads approximately 2.59 GB over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its delivery providers. They receive connection information, including your IP address and the requested model file. Ally does not include your Talk or Journey content in these requests. Their information handling is governed by their own policies, including Hugging Face's privacy policy. Download work can continue or resume in the background after you request it, subject to the connection setting you choose.

Human-support links open external services only when you choose them. A support share action opens another app with a generic support request. You decide whether to send it. External services have their own privacy practices.

Feedback and response reports

In Ally 1.0.1-beta4 (build 6) and later, Report response submits an optional AI response report inside Ally over HTTPS to Ally's operator-owned Supabase project. Supabase processes these reports for Ally; the database is hosted in East US (North Virginia). Reports help Ally support investigate unsafe responses and app problems. They are not sent to a cloud AI service for generation.

Before submission you see the exact selected Ally response, can edit or redact it, and can enter a comment. The report contains only that edited response, your comment, app version/build, and optionally your phone model and Android version. Device details and explicit submission consent are unchecked by default. No user prompt, full transcript, Journey record, profile/preferred-name field, location field, account/device identifier or attachment is added. Personal information may still appear in the selected response or comment; please remove unnecessary private details before consenting.

Protocol fields include a fresh random report ID, schema version and a hash of a random per-report deletion token. These are not persistent user or device identifiers. The server supplies a receipt timestamp only after a committed submission. A timeout is not a success receipt. Retry uses the same report ID to avoid duplicate reports. Cancel removes the pending text from the local draft; a request that already reached the server may still need deletion.

Supabase and its network providers receive connection metadata, including IP address, request time, user-agent/device network details and IP-derived approximate city/region/country information. Provider operational/security logs may contain this metadata. Ally's handlers do not log report text or deletion tokens; inspected provider invocation logs contained metadata, not report bodies. The project's current Free plan provides one day of API/database log retention, subject to provider operations and plan changes. Provider processing follows Supabase's privacy policy. Ally does not configure app analytics, advertising, remote transcript uploads or crash-report uploads.

Report content expires 90 days after server receipt. While the backend is active, the hourly purge removes expired rows at its next run (up to an additional hour), or report content is removed sooner through the in-app deletion endpoint or an operator deletion. Requests also purge expired rows before processing. After in-app deletion, a content-free receipt/token-hash record remains until the original expiry so deletion retries can be handled safely. The current Supabase Free plan can pause an inactive project; while paused, reporting/deletion and scheduled purges cannot run, and provider pause/restore copies can retain data beyond the app's expiry. Ally's operator must review the project daily, respond to pause warnings, and run the purge immediately on resume before review. Current provider documentation allows paused-project restoration for up to one year. This project has no operator-configured scheduled database backups or point-in-time recovery. Provider internal operational copies are governed by the provider's policies; app-level deletion is not a promise of immediate forensic erasure from every provider system. Do not create content-bearing report exports/backups without updating these practices and applying deletion/expiry records on restoration.

Report drafts and deletion receipts are encrypted on the phone using Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM. Pending draft text is removed after seven days when report controls next open; it is not uploaded automatically. If Ally is never reopened, that local expiry cannot run. Cancel removes pending draft text immediately. Receipts/deletion capability records remain locally for up to 91 days and are purged when report controls open. Uninstall or Clear storage removes these local records but does not delete reports already submitted to the server.

Use the receipt's Delete submitted report action or Help → Submitted reports. Keep your report ID if you may uninstall. If the receipt is lost or deletion cannot be confirmed, contact allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com with the report ID; Ally support can remove the report without returning its private content. Public users cannot read or directly write the report table. Only authorized operator dashboard users review reports; Supabase/provider operational access remains governed by their service practices.

Help → Send beta feedback remains a user-reviewed email draft. Older builds through 1.0.1-beta3 (build 5) also report responses by email. The email draft includes app version/build and your comment, with device details and one selected response optional. You choose whether to send in your email app. Opening the draft shares its contents with that app; its storage/draft synchronization follow its own policy. Sent email also includes your email address and the message information supplied by the email service. Email support records are separate from Supabase reports and are not subject to the 90-day report-table purge. Contact the support address to request deletion or ask about retention of a particular email. Copies outside Ally's control remain subject to those providers' policies.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your phone until you delete them. Talk → Chats provides a confirmed Delete all Talk history action that removes saved Talk messages, Talk memory records, saved generation records and older imported Talk chats. Journey and bounded runtime failure counters are retained. Remove downloaded model removes the model, its partial download and its compiled cache; history and Journey remain. Cancel a running download and wait for it to stop before removing the files. Talk requires another model download afterward.

Android Settings → Apps → Ally → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling Ally, removes Ally's private local data, preferences and model files. These actions cannot be undone through Ally. App backup and app-data cloud/device-transfer backup are disabled in this build; Ally offers no cloud restore. A cancelled download may retain a partial file until you resume it, remove the model files, clear storage or uninstall.

Deletion is app-level removal, not a guarantee of forensic erasure of flash storage. It does not erase screenshots, clipboard copies, email or other copies you deliberately shared outside Ally.

Screen, clipboard and notifications

Ally allows screenshots and screen recordings. Your keyboard, screen-access features or someone with device access can expose visible or typed content. Long-pressing a message copies it to the system clipboard. Clipboard protections and copies outside Ally follow Android/other-app behavior.

Optional reminders display generic Journey information according to your notification and lock-screen settings. Ally does not put Talk message text in its reminder notifications.

Changes and contact

Material changes to app data practices require an updated notice and, where appropriate, updated disclosures. Send privacy questions and requests to allyhelpseveryone@gmail.com. Do not use this address for emergency assistance.
